Pink noise has equal energy per octave, so a flat EQ shows a flat spectrum and every fader is easy to hear.

Equalizer

Low shelf, eight peaking bands, high shelf. 80 Hz to 10 kHz.

Checked and not checked

Checked by measurement: each fader's boost or cut lands on its band center within 1.5 dB, shelves hold their level past the corner, and rendered audio changes as the curve says.

Not checked: how it sounds. The Bell 1940 layout reuses the band edges from Dudley's vocoder (ten bands, lowest 0 to 250 Hz, the rest 300 Hz wide, to 2950 Hz), but a vocoder's band-pass filters are not the same as these peaking filters.
